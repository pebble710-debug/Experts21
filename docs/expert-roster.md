## How to read this

1. **[Roster summary](#roster-summary):** all experts in one table, grouped by lifecycle phase.
2. **[Expert profiles](#expert-profiles):** each expert's title, one-sentence responsibility, research basis, specific skills (used only or mainly by that expert), and shared skills (by ID).
3. **[Shared skill library](#shared-skill-library):** skills used by two or more experts, with a definition of each.
4. **[Shared skill → expert map](#shared-skill--expert-map):** for each shared skill, the experts that use it.
5. **[Coverage matrix](#coverage-matrix):** a grid of experts × shared skills.
6. **[Notes for Step 5](#notes-for-step-5-building-the-agents).**

The slugs (for example `launch-program-director`, `S04`) are suggested names for the agent and skill files in Step 5.

## Expert profiles

### Phase 1. Concept & Strategy

#### A01 — Launch Program Director

- **Slug:** `launch-program-director`
- **Responsibility:** Leads the expert team, owns the overall plan and timeline from concept to opening day, and settles conflicts between experts' recommendations.
- **Research basis:** README open questions; 04 timeline (9–15 months); 09 risk ranking
- **Specific skills:**
  - Breaking the launch into workstreams with owners
  - Keeping a decision log and open-questions list (site, space type, concept, funding, price position)
  - Go/no-go checks (lease signed, permits issued, DBPR inspection passed)
  - Combining experts' outputs into one plan and resolving disagreements
  - Owning the risk register (top 9 risks from 09-risks.md)
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S04 Unit economics & P&L modeling, S10 Critical-path scheduling, S12 Lease & permit-contingency review, S17 Hurricane & business-continuity planning, S24 Capital budgeting & contingency

#### A02 — Concept & Brand Strategist

- **Slug:** `concept-brand-strategist`
- **Responsibility:** Defines the café concept, positioning, and brand identity so it stands out from local roasters, incoming chains, and cafecito windows.
- **Research basis:** 03 positioning gaps; 08 positioning ideas; 02 segments
- **Specific skills:**
  - Positioning statement and brand architecture
  - Naming and trademark-screening brief (with the entity/legal expert)
  - Visual identity direction: logo, colors, packaging, store look
  - Concept format choice: seated café vs. grab-and-go vs. kiosk or pop-up test
  - Brand story built on Latin American origins and specialty versions of Cuban drinks
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S06 Price positioning vs. local anchors, S07 Neighborhood & demographic analysis, S08 Competitor tracking, S09 Bilingual & Miami cultural fluency, S19 Brand voice & content creation

#### A03 — Market & Customer Insights Analyst

- **Slug:** `market-customer-insights-analyst`
- **Responsibility:** Measures demand and defines target customer segments for each candidate neighborhood using demographic, tourism, and on-the-ground research.
- **Research basis:** 01 market; 02 segments and next-step research
- **Specific skills:**
  - Census ACS 5-year tract pulls and LEHD OnTheMap analysis
  - Survey design: 100+ intercept surveys and pop-up demand tests
  - Price-sensitivity testing (for example, $6 vs. $7 latte)
  - Tourism and visitor-flow analysis (GMCVB data, top origin markets)
  - Writing segment personas (office workers, condo residents, enthusiasts, tourists, Latin "upgraders", students)
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S06 Price positioning vs. local anchors, S07 Neighborhood & demographic analysis, S08 Competitor tracking, S09 Bilingual & Miami cultural fluency, S13 Seasonality & demand forecasting

#### A04 — Competitive Intelligence Analyst

- **Slug:** `competitive-intelligence-analyst`
- **Responsibility:** Maps and watches every competitor near candidate sites, from specialty roasters and new chains to Starbucks and ventanitas, and finds gaps to exploit.
- **Research basis:** 03 competitors and next steps
- **Specific skills:**
  - Building a competitor list (address, 12 oz latte price, hours, seats, Google rating and review count)
  - Mapping chain density (Starbucks, Dunkin', 7 Brew) within 0.5–1 mile
  - Profiling incoming chains (Black Sheep, The Coffee) and local growers (Crema Gourmet, Ludlow, Cortadito)
  - Mystery-shop visits: speed, quality, and service
  - Monthly scan of openings and closings
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S06 Price positioning vs. local anchors, S07 Neighborhood & demographic analysis, S08 Competitor tracking

### Phase 2. Finance & Legal Setup

#### A05 — Financial Modeling & Funding Strategist

- **Slug:** `financial-modeling-funding-strategist`
- **Responsibility:** Builds the startup budget, operating forecasts, and break-even analysis, and secures funding including 3–6 months of working capital.
- **Research basis:** 04 startup model; 05 sample P&L; 09 risk #9 undercapitalization
- **Specific skills:**
  - 3-statement financial model (P&L, cash flow, balance sheet)
  - Scenario and sensitivity analysis (rent, ticket, transactions/day, labor %)
  - SBA 7(a)/504 loan packages and lender pitch materials
  - Investor and partner deal structures
  - Cash-runway and working-capital planning for summer months
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S04 Unit economics & P&L modeling, S05 Recipe & drink costing, S06 Price positioning vs. local anchors, S11 Contract & vendor negotiation, S13 Seasonality & demand forecasting, S14 Florida wage & labor-cost math, S16 Commodity & input-cost monitoring, S24 Capital budgeting & contingency

#### A06 — Entity, Tax & Bookkeeping Specialist

- **Slug:** `entity-tax-bookkeeping-specialist`
- **Responsibility:** Sets up the business entity and tax accounts, and keeps the books, sales-tax filings, and payroll taxes correct and on time.
- **Research basis:** 06 items 1–2, 15; 05 taxes
- **Specific skills:**
  - Florida LLC formation via Sunbiz, annual report by May 1 (to avoid the $400 late fee), fictitious name filing
  - EIN, FL DOR sales-tax and reemployment-tax registration
  - 7% Miami-Dade sales-tax collection and filing
  - Checking leases for any taxable charges remaining after the Oct 1, 2025 rent-tax repeal
  - Chart of accounts, bookkeeping software, month-end close, and the county + city BTR renewal calendar (Oct 1 to Sept 30)
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S02 Primary-source verification, S03 Miami-Dade jurisdiction mapping, S04 Unit economics & P&L modeling, S14 Florida wage & labor-cost math

### Phase 3. Site & Build-Out

#### A07 — Site Selection & Commercial Real Estate Specialist

- **Slug:** `site-selection-real-estate-specialist`
- **Responsibility:** Finds and scores candidate locations, and keeps total occupancy cost at or below about 10–12% of realistic sales.
- **Research basis:** 05 rent table; 09 risk #1; 02 best-fit areas
- **Specific skills:**
  - Miami submarket rent knowledge (county average $50.27/sq ft; Wynwood $100+; Brickell ~$70; Coral Gables ~2.2% vacancy)
  - Screening for second-generation food space (existing grease trap, plumbing, hood)
  - Site scoring: visibility, foot traffic, parking, co-tenants, outdoor seating
  - Checking the FEMA flood zone and elevation of each site
  - Working with brokers and touring sites
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S03 Miami-Dade jurisdiction mapping, S04 Unit economics & P&L modeling, S07 Neighborhood & demographic analysis, S08 Competitor tracking, S12 Lease & permit-contingency review, S24 Capital budgeting & contingency

#### A08 — Lease Negotiation Specialist

- **Slug:** `lease-negotiation-specialist`
- **Responsibility:** Negotiates the lease (LOI to signed lease) to protect the business against high rent, delays, and personal liability.
- **Research basis:** 05 NNN and occupancy rule; 09 risk #1 mitigation
- **Specific skills:**
  - Letter of intent (LOI) drafting
  - Getting TI allowance and free rent during build-out
  - NNN/CAM caps, audit rights, and expense pass-through limits
  - Limiting the personal guarantee (burn-off, cap)
  - Permit contingency, exclusive-use, co-tenancy, renewal, and assignment clauses
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S03 Miami-Dade jurisdiction mapping, S04 Unit economics & P&L modeling, S11 Contract & vendor negotiation, S12 Lease & permit-contingency review, S24 Capital budgeting & contingency

#### A09 — Permitting & Zoning Expediter

- **Slug:** `permitting-zoning-expediter`
- **Responsibility:** Gets the site's zoning approval and every building, fire, sign, Certificate of Use, BTR, and sidewalk-café permit on the fastest legal path.
- **Research basis:** 06 checklist items 3–4, 9–11, 16–17; README open question 1
- **Specific skills:**
  - Zoning verification before the lease is signed (use allowed, parking, alcohol distance letter)
  - Building, fire, and sign permit applications and tracking inspections
  - City of Miami eStart / iPW portal, Certificate of Use, city + county BTR
  - Sidewalk-café permit (layout drawing, deposit, yearly renewal)
  - Comparing municipal rules (Miami vs. Miami Beach vs. Coral Gables)
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S02 Primary-source verification, S03 Miami-Dade jurisdiction mapping, S10 Critical-path scheduling, S12 Lease & permit-contingency review

#### A10 — Environmental & Utility Compliance Specialist

- **Slug:** `environmental-utility-compliance-specialist`
- **Responsibility:** Handles Miami-Dade DERM grease (FOG) rules, the water and sewer connection review, and impact fees, which are among the largest unknown costs.
- **Research basis:** 06 items 6–8; 04 grease-trap line ($0–$35k); 09 risk #3
- **Specific skills:**
  - DERM Grease Discharge Operating (GDO) permit applications
  - Grease trap or interceptor sizing, and checking existing traps against current rules
  - WASD connection-charge estimates ($1.39 per average daily gallon, 2025–26)
  - Impact-fee review for change of use
  - Pump-out schedule, DERM-licensed hauler, online reporting, and the maintenance log; tracking proposed Chapter 24 FOG changes
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S02 Primary-source verification, S03 Miami-Dade jurisdiction mapping, S10 Critical-path scheduling, S15 Food safety & sanitation standards, S22 Bar layout & equipment specification

#### A11 — Café Design & Build-Out Project Manager

- **Slug:** `design-buildout-project-manager`
- **Responsibility:** Manages the architect, engineers, and contractor to deliver the build-out on budget, on schedule, and up to code.

  - Architect and MEP-engineer coordination; signed and sealed drawings for permits and DBPR plan review

### Phase 4. Compliance & Risk

#### A12 — Food Safety & Licensing Compliance Specialist

- **Slug:** `food-safety-licensing-specialist`
- **Responsibility:** Gets and keeps the DBPR food service license, food-safety certifications, and operating licenses, and keeps the café ready for inspection.
- **Research basis:** 06 items 5, 12–14, 19–20; optional alcohol and roasting rows
- **Specific skills:**
  - DBPR food service license application (seating tier: $262 for 1–49 seats, plus $50 application fee) and opening inspection
  - DBPR plan review for spaces never licensed for food
  - Certified Food Protection Manager and food-handler training tracking (Rule 61C-4.023)
  - Mock inspections and corrective-action plans
  - Music licensing (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC); optional alcohol (2COP) or roasting/FDACS review
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S02 Primary-source verification, S03 Miami-Dade jurisdiction mapping, S15 Food safety & sanitation standards, S21 SOP & training design

- **Research basis:** 07 wage rules and hiring risks; 06 items 18–19
- **Specific skills:**

  - Tip-pool policy design under the FLSA
  - Employee handbook, offer letters, and classification (exempt vs. non-exempt, part-time vs. full-time)
  - Required federal and state posters
  - Tracking the 2028 inflation adjustment and new E-Verify bills
- **Shared skills:** S01 Research navigation & evidence labeling, S02 Primary-source verification, S14 Florida wage & labor-cost math, S23 Workforce eligibility compliance

## Shared skill library

Each shared skill should be written **once** in Step 5 and reused by every expert listed for it. That keeps facts like the $15 wage, the 7% sales tax, and the evidence labels the same across all agents.

| ID | Shared skill | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | **Research navigation & evidence labeling** | Find facts in `docs/`, keep the [CONFIRMED]/[REPORTED]/[ESTIMATE]/[UNVERIFIED] labels, and cite source links with dates. |
| S02 | **Primary-source verification** | Re-check fees, laws, and rates against the official source (DBPR, Sunbiz, Miami-Dade, FL DOR, flsenate.gov, BLS) before anyone acts on them. |
| S03 | **Miami-Dade jurisdiction mapping** | Work out which rules apply at a given address: City of Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral, another municipality, or unincorporated county. |
| S04 | **Unit economics & P&L modeling** | Model revenue (transactions × ticket), COGS, labor %, occupancy %, and break-even (about 150–170 transactions/day in the current model). |
| S05 | **Recipe & drink costing** | Cost each item by dose, milk, cup, and lid (for example, a 12 oz latte costs about $1.20–$1.60) and set target cost percentages. |
| S06 | **Price positioning vs. local anchors** | Set prices between the ~$2 colada and $4.18 average hot coffee and specialty drinks at $6+; design price tests. |
| S07 | **Neighborhood & demographic analysis** | Use ACS tract data, LEHD daytime workers, and foot-traffic data to judge demand near a site. |
| S08 | **Competitor tracking** | Keep a list of cafés near each site (price, hours, rating) and track openings and closings (Miami New Times, What Now Miami). |
| S09 | **Bilingual & Miami cultural fluency** | Write and speak in both Spanish and English, and apply cafecito culture (ventanitas, coladas, 3:05 break) to the brand and service. |
| S10 | **Critical-path scheduling** | Manage the 9–15 month opening timeline and the dependencies between lease, permits, DERM, build-out, hiring, and launch. |
| S11 | **Contract & vendor negotiation** | Negotiate terms such as TI allowance and free rent, fixed-price roaster contracts, equipment leases, POS and delivery-app fees, and insurance quotes. |
| S12 | **Lease & permit-contingency review** | Review lease clauses: permit contingency, CAM caps, personal guarantee, renewal and exit, and use clause. |
| S13 | **Seasonality & demand forecasting** | Forecast the Dec–Apr peak, summer slowdown, hurricane season, and event spikes (Art Basel, Ultra, F1). |
| S14 | **Florida wage & labor-cost math** | Apply the $15.00 minimum and $11.98 tipped wage ($3.02 tip credit), 12–20% payroll burden, and a 250–320 hrs/week labor budget. |
| S15 | **Food safety & sanitation standards** | Apply DBPR rules: Certified Food Protection Manager, handler training every 3 years, inspections, and grease-trap logs. |
| S16 | **Commodity & input-cost monitoring** | Track arabica futures (~$2.75/lb, Nov 2026), tariff policy, and milk and packaging prices, and flag when menu prices need changing. |
| S17 | **Hurricane & business-continuity planning** | Plan for storms (June 1–Nov 30): flood zone, generator or power plan, cold-chain loss, staff communication, and reopening. |
| S18 | **POS & sales data analytics** | Read POS data: average ticket, transactions by hour, item mix, repeat rate, loyalty and BOGO results. |
| S19 | **Brand voice & content creation** | Write and design on-brand copy, menus, and visual content for the "Miami-made specialty" position. |
| S20 | **Customer feedback & review management** | Monitor and reply to Google and Yelp reviews, run surveys, and turn feedback into fixes. |
| S21 | **SOP & training design** | Write step-by-step standard operating procedures, checklists, and training modules that staff can follow. |
| S22 | **Bar layout & equipment specification** | Specify espresso, grinders, ice (mostly iced drinks), water filtration, refrigeration, and their utility and grease needs. |
| S23 | **Workforce eligibility compliance** | Handle I-9, E-Verify (required at 25+ employees, FL Stat. 448.095), and the effect of TPS changes on staffing. |
| S24 | **Capital budgeting & contingency** | Build cost budgets with low and high cases and a 10–20% contingency (total about $230k–$785k), and track actual spend against them. |

## Notes for Step 5 (building the agents)

- **Orchestration:** A01 Launch Program Director coordinates the other experts. All other experts report to A01 and hand work to one another through it.
- **Shared skills to build first:** the most-used skills give the most consistency: S01 (24), S04 (7), S06 (7), S09 (7), S11 (7), S13 (7).
- **Facts to keep in one place:** dollar amounts, rates, and dates (wages, fees, rents, tax rates) should live in the shared skills or in `docs/`, not be copied into each agent, so they can be updated once. Several are [UNVERIFIED] and must be re-checked (S02) before use.
- **Main handoffs:** A07 → A08 → A09/A10 → A11 (site → lease → permits → build); A05 ↔ A07/A08/A11 (budget checks); A15 → A16 → A17 (supply → drink menu → food menu and buying); A18 → A19 → A20 (hire → run → serve); A21 feeds POS data (S18) to A05, A19, A22, A23, A24.
- **Professional review:** A06, A08, A09, A12, A13, and A14 produce planning drafts. Their output should be reviewed by a licensed Florida attorney, CPA, insurance broker, or permit expediter before anyone acts on it.
