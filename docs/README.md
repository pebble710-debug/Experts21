# Miami Specialty Coffee Shop — Research Index

Background research for opening an independent **specialty coffee shop in Miami, Florida (Miami-Dade County)**. It is written as shared context for a team of expert agents (market, finance, legal/permits, operations/HR, marketing, risk).

- **Research date:** 2026-11-23 (all sources accessed on this date unless noted)
- **Scope:** A single-unit, ~800–1,500 sq ft espresso-forward café with light food. No roasting on site and no alcohol, unless noted.
- **Status:** Desk research only. Nothing here has been checked with a landlord, contractor, attorney, CPA, or the permitting agencies.

## Open questions for the expert team

1. **Site:** Which neighborhood and which municipality? City of Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, and unincorporated county each have their own zoning, Certificate of Use, sidewalk-café, and business-tax rules.
2. **Space:** Is it a second-generation food space with an existing grease trap and plumbing? This is the biggest swing factor in startup cost and timeline.
3. **Concept:** Seated café or grab-and-go? Food program? Alcohol (needs a separate state license and zoning review)? Roasting on site (air permit and ventilation)?
4. **Funding:** How much capital, and from where? The model assumes 3–6 months of working capital.
5. **Price position:** Where to sit between the ~$2 colada and $7+ specialty drinks.
