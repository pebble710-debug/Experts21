- **NNN:** Most Miami retail leases are triple-net. The tenant also pays its share of property taxes, insurance, and common-area costs (CAM), often **$15–$30/sq ft** more. [ESTIMATE]
- **Sales tax on rent is gone.** Florida repealed its business rent tax (HB 7031) for occupancy periods starting on or after **Oct 1, 2025**. It had been 2% since mid-2024. Check each lease for other taxable charges. [CONFIRMED]
- **Rule of thumb:** Total occupancy cost (base rent + NNN) should stay under about **10–12% of sales**. At $90/sq ft all-in on 1,200 sq ft ($108k/yr), you would need about **$900k–$1.1M** in annual sales. [ESTIMATE]

|---|---|---|
| Total COGS (coffee, milk, food, packaging) | 25–35% of revenue; target under 30% | [ESTIMATE] industry guides |
| Australian Tax Office small-café benchmark | 36–38% of turnover | [REPORTED] (not U.S., but a useful check) |
| Arabica futures | about $2.75/lb (Nov 16, 2026), down about 32% year over year | [REPORTED] |
| Specialty wholesale roasted coffee | Often **$10–$18/lb** depending on origin and volume | [ESTIMATE]; get roaster quotes |

**Drink cost example [ESTIMATE]:** 18 g dose at $14/lb roasted is about $0.56. Add milk (~$0.35–$0.60, more for oat), cup, lid, and sleeve (~$0.25–$0.40). A 12 oz latte costs roughly **$1.20–$1.60** to make. At a $6.00 price, that is about a 20–27% drink cost. Food usually has a higher cost (30–40%).

## Sample annual P&L, one café [ESTIMATE]

Assumptions: 1,200 sq ft; open 7 days a week, 360 days; average ticket $9.

| Line | Conservative | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Transactions per day | 180 | 280 |
| **Revenue** | **$583k** | **$907k** |
| COGS (30%) | $175k | $272k |
| Labor incl. burden (34% / 30%) | $198k | $272k |
| Rent + NNN (fixed, $90/sq ft all-in) | $108k | $108k |
| Utilities, insurance, repairs | $45k | $50k |
| Card fees, software, marketing, admin (8%) | $47k | $73k |
| **Operating profit before debt service, owner pay, and depreciation** | **≈ $10k (≈2%)** | **≈ $132k (≈15%)** |

Industry guides put break-even at **80–100 transactions/day** and profitability at **150–200+**. Miami's high rent pushes break-even higher. In this model it is about **150–170 transactions/day** (fixed costs of ~$153k divided by a 28–32% contribution margin at a $9 ticket). **The finance agent should rebuild this model with real lease terms.**
