# 6. Permits and Licenses

Research date: 2026-11-23. Labels are defined in [README](README.md#evidence-labels-used-in-every-file).

> **Not legal advice.** Requirements depend on the **exact address**. Miami-Dade has 34 municipalities, and the City of Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral, and others each have their own zoning, Certificate of Use, sidewalk-café, and business-tax rules. This list is based on the **City of Miami** plus county and state rules. Confirm every item with the agency or a local permit expediter before you sign a lease.

## Practical notes

- **Do zoning and DERM checks before signing the lease.** Add a lease clause that lets you exit if permits are not issued (a permit contingency).
- **A second-generation food space can save time**, but **check that the existing grease trap meets current DERM rules** and that the GDO permit can transfer or be re-issued.
- **Hiring a local permit expediter** (about $3k–$15k) often pays for itself through shorter delays. [ESTIMATE]
- City of Miami **eStart** is the city's guide to opening a business: https://miami.gov/estart/Guide-to-Opening-a-Business-with-eStart
