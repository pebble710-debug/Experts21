# 4. Startup Costs

Research date: 2026-11-23. Labels are defined in [README](README.md#evidence-labels-used-in-every-file).

> **Read this first:** Almost every number here is an **[ESTIMATE]**. The national ranges come from vendor and POS guides (Toast, 7shifts, DoorDash, Bellwether, and others). The Miami column is our own model, adjusted for local rents and costs. The only **[CONFIRMED]** amounts are the government fees, which are listed in [06-permits-licenses.md](06-permits-licenses.md). Replace the estimates with real quotes from landlords, contractors, and equipment dealers.

## Miami model: ~1,200 sq ft seated café, about 30 seats [ESTIMATE]

Assumptions: a second-generation restaurant or café space if possible; leased (not bought); no on-site roasting; no alcohol; a simple food program (pastries and toasts, no hood). **Low** = second-generation space with a usable grease trap and small changes. **High** = a raw "white box" or a space that was never licensed for food (needs plan review, plumbing, a grease interceptor, and possibly impact or connection fees).

| Category | Low | High | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Design: architect, MEP engineer, permit expediter | $10,000 | $40,000 | Signed and sealed drawings are needed for building permits and DBPR plan review. |

## How to lower costs

- **Lease a second-generation café space.** This can save $50k–$100k+ in build-out and may avoid a new grease interceptor and change-of-use fees. Confirm with DERM and the water department what is already permitted.
- **Negotiate a tenant improvement (TI) allowance and free rent** for the build-out period (common in new mixed-use buildings).
- **Lease or finance the espresso machine,** or get it through a roaster's wholesale deal (some roasters loan equipment if you buy their beans).
- **Start with a lighter format** (kiosk, pop-up, or cart) to test demand before committing to a full lease.

## Timeline [ESTIMATE]

National guides suggest about 6–12 months from planning to opening. In Miami, plan for **9–15 months** if the space needs plan review, building permits, and DERM grease review. Delays here directly raise costs (rent paid with no revenue).
