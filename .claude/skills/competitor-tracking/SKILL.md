# Competitor tracking (S08)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Keep a list of cafés near each site (price, hours, rating) and track openings and closings (Miami New Times, What Now Miami).

## When to use

Use when assessing a site, positioning the concept, setting prices, or doing monthly market monitoring.

## Procedure

1. Start from the docs/03 list (local specialty, incoming chains, mass chains, ventanitas).
2. For each site, list every café within 0.5–1 mile: name, address, 12 oz latte price, hours, seats, Google rating and review count, and date checked.
3. Mark status: open / announced / closed. Check announced openings because press dates slip.
4. Each month, scan Miami New Times, What Now Miami, Eater Miami, and Resident for openings and closings.
5. Summarize the threats and gaps for the strategy, pricing, and site experts.

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- Key players: Panther Coffee (Sunset Harbour café closed 2026-02-28), Per'La, Pasion del Cielo, Ludlow, Crema Gourmet (25+), Cortadito, Sunshine, Naughty; outside chains Black Sheep (UK) and The Coffee (Brazil); 7 Brew possible. [REPORTED] docs/03.
- Starbucks and Dunkin' locations were not counted in the research. Count them per site.

## Output

A table: `Name | Type | Distance | Latte price | Rating (n) | Status | Checked on`.

## Guardrails

- Mark any competitor detail older than 3 months as needing a re-check.
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
