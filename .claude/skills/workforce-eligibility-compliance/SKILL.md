# Workforce eligibility compliance (S23)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Handle I-9, E-Verify (required at 25+ employees, FL Stat. 448.095), and the effect of TPS changes on staffing.

## When to use

Use when hiring, onboarding, auditing personnel files, or planning staffing in light of immigration-policy changes.

## Procedure

1. Complete Form I-9 for every hire within the federal deadlines. Keep files organized for audits.
2. E-Verify is required under FL Stat. 448.095 for private employers with 25 or more employees [CONFIRMED]. It is voluntary below 25. Ask counsel whether to use it anyway.
3. Track legislation: universal E-Verify bills died in 2025 (HB 955) and 2026 (HB 197). [CONFIRMED] Check each session.
4. Plan for the labor-pool impact of the end of TPS (Supreme Court ruling 2026-06-25; FRLA estimate ~93,000 FL hospitality workers). [REPORTED]
5. Apply the same rules to every applicant. Never discriminate based on national origin or citizenship status.

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- FL Stat. 448.095: https://flsenate.gov/laws/statutes/2025/448.095
- Evidence of staff losses is mostly from individual businesses (for example, Chef Creole). [REPORTED]

## Output

A compliance checklist plus a staffing-risk note.

## Guardrails

- Immigration law questions go to a licensed immigration or employment attorney.
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
