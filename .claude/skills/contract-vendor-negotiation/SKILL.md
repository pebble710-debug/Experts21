# Contract & vendor negotiation (S11)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Negotiate terms such as TI allowance and free rent, fixed-price roaster contracts, equipment leases, POS and delivery-app fees, and insurance quotes.

## When to use

Use when comparing quotes, drafting a term sheet, or negotiating price, terms, or risk with any counterparty.

## Procedure

1. Get at least 3 comparable quotes where possible, using a standard spec.
2. Know your walk-away point (BATNA) and your target, and list the terms that matter beyond price (term, minimums, escalators, service levels, exit).
3. Use market benchmarks from docs/ (rent, TI and free rent, card fees 2.5–3.5%, delivery commissions 15–30%, roaster equipment-loan deals). All [ESTIMATE]/[REPORTED].
4. Write the agreed terms in a term sheet and send legal terms for professional review.
5. Record what was agreed and when it renews.

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- Lease asks: TI allowance, free rent during build-out, CAM caps, limited personal guarantee, permit contingency. docs/05, 09.
- Roaster deals: 6–12 month fixed-price contracts; some roasters loan equipment or provide training. [ESTIMATE] docs/04, 09.

## Output

A quote comparison: `Vendor | Price | Key terms | Risks | Score`, then a recommended ask.

## Guardrails

- Not legal advice. Contracts need attorney review before signing.
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
