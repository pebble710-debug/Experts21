# Brand voice & content creation (S19)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Write and design on-brand copy, menus, and visual content for the "Miami-made specialty" position.

## When to use

Use when writing anything public: menus, website text, social captions, press releases, signs, packaging, job ads.

## Procedure

1. Use the positioning from docs/08: Miami-made specialty, bilingual by default, built for the heat, a neighborhood third place.
2. Tone: warm, confident, local. Tell the story of origins (Latin American coffees) without jargon overload.
3. Write in English and Spanish (with the bilingual skill). Keep claims true. Never claim awards, sourcing, or certifications that are not real.
4. For visuals, write a brief: shot list, style, and the drinks to feature (iced, Cuban-style specialty).
5. Check against the brand guide (once A02 creates one).

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- Discovery: social media is about 35% of coffee-shop discovery. [REPORTED] Toast.
- Gap to own: specialty versions of Cuban and Latin drinks. [ESTIMATE] docs/03.

## Output

The copy itself, plus a note of the assumptions behind any claim.

## Guardrails

- No unverifiable claims ("best in Miami", "organic", "direct trade") without evidence.
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
