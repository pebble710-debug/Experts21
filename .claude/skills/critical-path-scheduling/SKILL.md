# Critical-path scheduling (S10)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Manage the 9–15 month opening timeline and the dependencies between lease, permits, DERM, build-out, hiring, and launch.

## When to use

Use when building or updating the launch schedule, judging a delay, or deciding the order of tasks.

## Procedure

1. List the milestones: concept → funding → site → zoning check → LOI and lease (with permit contingency) → design → permits and DERM/WASD → build-out → inspections → DBPR licence → hiring and training → soft opening → grand opening.
2. For each milestone, note the owner (agent), duration ([ESTIMATE]), predecessors, and what is waiting on an agency.
3. Find the critical path (usually permits, DERM grease review, and build-out) and the slack.
4. Turn the dates into a cost: each month of delay costs a month of rent with no revenue.
5. Flag decisions that must happen before the lease is signed.

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- Planning to opening: 6–12 months nationally; plan 9–15 months in Miami if plan review, building permits, and DERM review are needed. [ESTIMATE] docs/04.
- Launch marketing starts 8–12 weeks before opening. [ESTIMATE] docs/08.

## Output

A milestone table: `Milestone | Owner | Depends on | Est. duration | Status | Risk`.

## Guardrails

- Agency review times are estimates unless the agency has published them.
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
