# Bar layout & equipment specification (S22)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Specify espresso, grinders, ice (mostly iced drinks), water filtration, refrigeration, and their utility and grease needs.

## When to use

Use when designing the bar, choosing equipment, coordinating the build-out, or planning maintenance.

## Procedure

1. Size for demand: peak drinks per hour (from the forecast) → groups, grinders, and a second bar station.
2. Plan for mostly iced drinks: ice-machine capacity, cold-brew system, and refrigeration.
3. List utility needs for each item: electrical (voltage, amps), water, filtration, drains, and any grease load (for the DERM sizing).
4. Workflow: order → payment → espresso → milk → handoff. Minimize steps and crossing paths. Plan for mobile pickup.
5. Get a maintenance plan, service vendor, spare parts, and warranty for each item.

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- Espresso machine (2–3 group) $12k–$25k; grinders $4k–$12k; brewers, filtration, ice, and blenders $6k–$15k; refrigeration $8k–$25k. [ESTIMATE] docs/04.
- A large ice machine matters in Miami (mostly iced drinks). [ESTIMATE] docs/04.

## Output

An equipment schedule: `Item | Model/spec | Qty | Utilities | Cost | Lead time | Service`.

## Guardrails

- Prices are estimates. Get dealer quotes.
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
