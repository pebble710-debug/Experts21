# POS & sales data analytics (S18)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Read POS data: average ticket, transactions by hour, item mix, repeat rate, loyalty and BOGO results.

## When to use

Use after launch (or in pop-up tests) for KPI reviews, staffing to demand, menu engineering, and measuring campaigns.

## Procedure

1. Define the KPIs: revenue, transactions, average ticket, items per ticket, sales per labor hour, labor %, COGS %, repeat-customer rate, loyalty sign-ups.
2. Compare to targets: ticket $9 in the model; break-even about 150–170 transactions/day. [ESTIMATE]
3. Look at the data by hour and weekday to set schedules (7–10 a.m. peak). [ESTIMATE]
4. Menu engineering: popularity × margin, and find items to cut or reprice.
5. Measure promotions (BOGO, sign-up offers) by incremental visits, not just redemptions.

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- Industry ticket benchmarks $5–$8 typical; $7–$10+ target. [ESTIMATE]
- Loyalty is the main choice factor for only about 1% of customers; a sign-up offer persuades 28%. [REPORTED] Toast.

## Output

A KPI table: `KPI | This period | Target | Trend | Action`.

## Guardrails

- Before launch there is no real data. Label any projection as an [ESTIMATE].
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
