# SOP & training design (S21)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Write step-by-step standard operating procedures, checklists, and training modules that staff can follow.

## When to use

Use when creating opening and closing checklists, drink specs, cleaning schedules, compliance routines, onboarding plans, or training.

## Procedure

1. Name the task, the owner, the frequency, and the standard.
2. Write numbered steps with tools, quantities, times, and the reason for each critical step. Keep each SOP to one page.
3. Add the check: how a lead confirms the task was done right (log, photo, taste test).
4. Turn the SOPs into training: demo → practice → sign-off. Track certifications (food manager, handler).
5. Keep versions and dates, and review SOPs every quarter or when equipment or rules change.

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- Required: Certified Food Protection Manager; handler training every 3 years. [CONFIRMED] docs/06.
- Barista training options: SCA Barista Skills modules or roaster-led training. [ESTIMATE] docs/07.

## Output

The SOP in this format: Title / Owner / When / Steps / Standard / Check / Version.

## Guardrails

- Compliance steps must match the current DBPR and DERM requirements. Flag anything you could not verify.
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
