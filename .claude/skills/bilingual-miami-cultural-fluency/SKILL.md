# Bilingual & Miami cultural fluency (S09)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Write and speak in both Spanish and English, and apply cafecito culture (ventanitas, coladas, 3:05 break) to the brand and service.

## When to use

Use when writing customer-facing copy, menus, signs, job posts, or social content, or when designing service or drinks for Miami customers.

## Procedure

1. Default to bilingual (Spanish/English) for anything customers or staff will read. Have a native speaker review Spanish copy.
2. Use the correct terms: cafecito/café cubano, cortadito, colada (shared, with thimble cups), café con leche, ventanita.
3. Respect the culture. Present specialty versions as an upgrade that honors the tradition, not as a correction of it.
4. Consider other community languages by neighborhood (Portuguese, Haitian Creole) where relevant.
5. Use local moments: the 3:05 p.m. cafecito break, Art Basel, and local holidays.

- The City of Miami proclaimed 3:05 p.m. cafecito time. [REPORTED]

## Output

Provide copy in English and Spanish side by side when relevant.

## Guardrails

- Avoid stereotypes. Do not invent cultural "facts".
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
