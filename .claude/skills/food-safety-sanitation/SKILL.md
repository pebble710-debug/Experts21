# Food safety & sanitation standards (S15)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Apply DBPR rules: Certified Food Protection Manager, handler training every 3 years, inspections, and grease-trap logs.

## When to use

Use when writing SOPs, training staff, designing the bar or kitchen, preparing for an inspection, or handling a food-safety incident.

## Procedure

1. Make sure there is a Certified Food Protection Manager (accredited exam) whose proof is available to DBPR on request. It is safest to certify every shift lead. [CONFIRMED] requirement (Rule 61C-4.023).
2. Train all food employees with a DBPR-approved program and renew every 3 years [CONFIRMED]. New staff within 60 days [REPORTED].
3. Keep daily logs: refrigeration temperatures, sanitizer concentration, cleaning, and the grease-trap pump-out log (DERM).
4. Run monthly mock inspections against DBPR criteria and fix findings.
5. Code items to design for: hand sinks, a three-compartment sink, and the ADA restroom (with the build-out expert).
