# Florida wage & labor-cost math (S14)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Apply the $15.00 minimum and $11.98 tipped wage ($3.02 tip credit), 12–20% payroll burden, and a 250–320 hrs/week labor budget.

## When to use

Use when pricing labor, building schedules or budgets, setting pay, or checking wage compliance.

## Procedure

1. Use the current rates: $15.00/hr minimum and $11.98/hr tipped cash wage (tip credit $3.02) since 2026-09-30. [CONFIRMED] The next change is inflation-indexed, effective 2028-01-01. [REPORTED]
2. Tipped employees must reach $15.00/hr including tips, and need the federal tip-credit notice.
3. Loaded cost = wages × (1 + burden). Burden is about 12–20% (FICA 7.65%, FUTA/SUTA, workers' comp). [ESTIMATE]
4. Hours budget: the docs/07 plan is 8–11 people, 350–450 hrs/week, which is too high for $600k–$900k sales. Target about 250–320 hrs/week with the owner working shifts. [ESTIMATE]
5. Check that labor stays around 25–32% of revenue. [ESTIMATE]

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- BLS May 2025 Miami SOC 35-3023 median $14.23/hr, excluding tips. [REPORTED] This is below the current $15 minimum, so market data understates current pay.
- Example: 400 hrs × $16.50 × 52 ≈ $343k wages; +15% ≈ $395k. [ESTIMATE] docs/07.

## Output

A table: `Role | Hours/wk | Rate | Weekly | Annual loaded`, then labor % of revenue.

## Guardrails

- Wage law questions beyond these rates (overtime exemptions, tip pools) go to the employment-law expert and counsel.
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
