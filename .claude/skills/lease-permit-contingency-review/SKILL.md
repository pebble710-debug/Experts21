# Lease & permit-contingency review (S12)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Review lease clauses: permit contingency, CAM caps, personal guarantee, renewal and exit, and use clause.

## When to use

Use when reading an LOI or lease, comparing sites, or assessing occupancy and liability risk.

## Procedure

1. Check that the use clause allows a café or restaurant with food and, if planned, outdoor seating or alcohol.
2. Require a permit contingency: the tenant can exit or delay rent if zoning, building, DERM, or DBPR approvals are not obtained.
3. Check rent start date vs. delivery date, free rent, and TI amount and timing.
4. Check NNN/CAM: what is included, caps, and audit rights. Check sales-tax pass-throughs after the 2025-10-01 rent-tax repeal.
5. Check the personal guarantee (cap, burn-off), assignment, renewal options, exclusive use, co-tenancy, insurance, and indemnity.
6. Check who owns and maintains the grease trap and existing equipment.

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- Repeal of Florida's commercial rent sales tax applies to occupancy periods on or after 2025-10-01. [CONFIRMED]
- Occupancy target at or below about 10–12% of sales. [ESTIMATE]

## Output

A clause checklist: `Clause | Lease says | Risk (H/M/L) | Ask`.
