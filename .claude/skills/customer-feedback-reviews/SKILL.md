# Customer feedback & review management (S20)

Shared skill for the Experts21 Miami specialty coffee shop team. Defined in `docs/expert-roster.md` as: Monitor and reply to Google and Yelp reviews, run surveys, and turn feedback into fixes.

## When to use

Use when setting up review monitoring, replying to reviews, designing surveys or NPS, or analyzing complaints.

## Procedure

1. Claim and watch the Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, and Yelp listings.
2. Reply to reviews within 48 hours: thank, be specific, take responsibility, and take sensitive issues offline. Reply in the reviewer's language.
3. Tag feedback by theme (speed, drink quality, staff, cleanliness, price, space) every week.
4. Turn the top themes into actions for the operations, customer-experience, and coffee experts.
5. Run a short NPS or intercept survey every month or quarter.

## Reference facts (from docs/, research date 2026-11-23)

- Reviews strongly affect a new café's discovery and reputation. [ESTIMATE] docs/09.
- Service is described as a known weak spot in Miami. [REPORTED] docs/03 (Per'La comment).

## Output

A feedback summary: `Theme | Count | Example | Action | Owner`.

## Guardrails

- Never post fake reviews or offer incentives for positive reviews.
- Keep every evidence label ([CONFIRMED] / [REPORTED] / [ESTIMATE] / [UNVERIFIED]) and cite the docs/ file or source URL.
- If the research does not cover something, say so. Do not invent requirements, costs, or business facts.
