# Sample Brickell Coffee Shop Lease Offer

**Document type:** Fictional landlord proposal for AI agent testing  
**Location:** Brickell, Miami, Florida  
**Purpose:** Evaluate proposed lease terms for a specialty coffee shop.

## Proposed Terms

- Space: 1,200 square feet
- Base rent: $78 per square foot annually
- Lease structure: Triple Net (NNN)
- Lease term: 5 years
- Annual rent increases: 3%
- Security deposit: 3 months of base rent
- Tenant improvement allowance: $0
- Personal guarantee: Required for the entire lease term
- Permitted use: Coffee shop, subject to landlord approval and government permits
- Grease trap: Not installed; tenant responsible for determining requirements and installation
- Opening deadline: Within 120 days of signing
- Early termination: Not permitted

## Landlord's Position

The landlord considers the location desirable because of pedestrian traffic and nearby offices. The landlord is unwilling to offer free rent or contribute to construction costs.

## Questions for the Lease Expert

1. Should we accept this offer as written?
2. What are the three most important terms to negotiate?
3. What financial and regulatory risks need to be reviewed before signing?

**Note:** This is an entirely fictional document created for classroom testing. It is not an actual lease offer.
